# Verification register

Every legal statement the kit relies on, where it comes from, and how far it has been checked. Read on
**24 October 2026**; the statements added in version 1.2.0 (A20 onwards, France, the Netherlands, Ireland, Austria,
Belgium) on **6 November 2026**. EUR-Lex, Légifrance and the RIS refuse automated reads, so those texts were read
through the official open-data services of the same publishers, named in each row.

**Status key**

- **Verified** - read in the act as published: the Official Journal of the EU, or the national official legal database
  or its official open-data service.
- **Official copy** - read on an official government service that reproduces the act, not the legal database itself.
- **Sources agree** - two or more independent secondary sources say the same.
- **Single source** - one secondary source.
- **Interpretation** - the text is read, but how it applies to a case is a judgement the register makes and names.
- **Background** - general knowledge of the practice, not checked in this session.

## What this kit is not

- **Not legal advice**, and not an assessment of real systems: the organisation is real only in the sense that it is
  the one analysed in the other projects of the portfolio; its AI systems and the answers about them are invented.
- **Not the provider's assessment.** Where a system is not high-risk under Article 6(3), the provider documents why;
  the register records the employer's reading and that the document should be on file.

## Known gaps

- The Commission's guidelines on the classification of high-risk systems (Art. 6(5)) and on prohibited practices were
  not reread; where they give an HR example, it overrides the register's reading of a boundary case.
- National labour law beyond the rules listed (collective agreements, data protection authority guidance) is not
  covered.
- Ireland's national law implementing the AI Act, and any duty it adds, was not found in the sources read; its status
  should be checked before the Irish section is relied on.
- The AI Office's Article 27(5) questionnaire template and the Commission's practical examples for Article 4 were not
  read; where they exist they shape the FRIA drafts and the literacy plan.
- Thresholds of size in the new countries are stated, not computed: the register holds no headcount there.
