# Questions for the supplier

> Generated from the register by `src/build_deliverables.py`; change the answers in `data/ai_systems.csv` and run `python src/run_all.py`. A dry run on invented systems, not legal advice.

What the employer can only do with the supplier's help, system by system: the instructions for use, the facts behind the notices to workers and the data protection impact assessment, the logs, and the warning before a design change that would end the grace period of Article 111(2). Send before signing or at renewal; keep the answers with the register.

## S01 · CV screening and ranking

*High-risk. 14 questions.*

1. **Send the instructions for use, with every item Article 13(3) lists** (Art. 13(2)-(3); 26(1)). Using the system as instructed is the first deployer duty.
2. **State the accuracy, robustness and cybersecurity levels the system was tested against, with the metrics, and the circumstances known to affect them** (Art. 13(3)(b)(ii)-(iii)). Monitoring, the DPIA and the Italian and Spanish notices need them.
3. **Give the system's performance for the groups of people it is used on** (Art. 13(3)(b)(v)). The check for discriminatory effects starts here.
4. **Describe the human oversight measures and the tools that help interpret the output** (Art. 13(3)(d); 14; 26(2)). The named overseers are trained on them.
5. **Specify the input data the system expects, and the data it was trained, validated and tested on** (Art. 13(3)(b)(vi); 26(4)). Input data must be relevant and representative.
6. **Explain how we collect, store and read the logs, and confirm they can be kept at least six months** (Art. 12; 13(3)(f); 26(6)). Log retention is a deployer duty.
7. **List the changes pre-determined at conformity assessment and commit to tell us before any significant design change** (Art. 13(3)(c); 111(2)). A significant change ends the grace period of Art. 111(2).
8. **Name the contact and the procedure for reporting risks and serious incidents** (Art. 26(5); 72; 73). The employer must inform the provider without undue delay.
9. **Provide the information we need for the data protection impact assessment** (Art. 13; 26(9); GDPR Art. 35). The DPIA is built on the provider's information.
10. **Explain what the system can show to explain an individual output** (Art. 13(3)(b)(iv); 86). The person concerned can ask for an explanation.
11. **Confirm the conformity assessment, the EU declaration of conformity, the CE marking and the registration in the EU database** (Art. 16(f)-(i); 47-49). A public employer may not use an unregistered system (Art. 26(8)).
12. **Confirm the system does not infer emotions, and will not without telling us first** (Art. 5(1)(f)). Emotion recognition at work is prohibited.
13. **Provide the logic, main parameters and data categories the national notices to workers require** (IT D.lgs. 152/1997 art. 1-bis co. 2; ES ET art. 64.4.d; AT ArbVG §91(2)). The employer writes the notices; the supplier holds the facts.
14. **Provide training material for the people who will operate and use it** (Art. 4). AI literacy measures for staff.
