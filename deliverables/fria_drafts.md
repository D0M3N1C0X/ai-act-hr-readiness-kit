# Fundamental rights impact assessments: drafts

> Generated from the register by `src/build_deliverables.py`; change the answers in `data/ai_systems.csv` and run `python src/run_all.py`. A dry run on invented systems, not legal advice.

**Status for this deployer (private employer):** not owed. Article 27(1) applies to bodies governed by public law, private entities providing public services, and deployers of credit and life and health insurance systems. These drafts are voluntary: they ask the questions a works council, a data protection authority or a court will ask, and they turn into the real assessment if the deployer changes.

Each draft follows the six elements of Article 27(1). Where the data protection impact assessment already meets an element, the assessment may cross-refer to it or include the relevant part (Art. 27(4) as replaced by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744); the third column names the section of a GDPR Article 35(7) assessment that usually holds it. Text in brackets is for the employer to complete.
