# AI literacy plan

> Generated from the register by `src/build_deliverables.py`; change the answers in `data/ai_systems.csv` and run `python src/run_all.py`. A dry run on invented systems, not legal advice.

Article 4, as replaced by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, asks providers and deployers to take measures to support the AI literacy of their staff and of others who operate and use AI systems on their behalf, taking into account their technical knowledge, experience, education and training, the context of use, and the people the systems are used on. It does not require any level to be guaranteed.

**Evidence to keep:** Date, material and attendance for each module and person: the measures taken, not a level reached (Art. 4(1) as replaced by Reg. 2026/1744).

## Modules

### L01 · Working with this system

- **For:** Everyone who operates or uses it
- **Covers:** What it does and does not do, its known limits, when not to rely on it, how to report a problem
- **Systems (11):** CV screening and ranking, Targeted job advertising, Interview scheduling assistant, Customer service performance analytics, Shift scheduling optimiser, Attrition-risk model, Pay review recommender, HR service chatbot, Learning recommendations, Writing and translation assistant, Internal mobility matching

### L02 · Overseeing a high-risk system

- **For:** The named overseers
- **Covers:** Reading the output, automation bias, the authority to override or stop it, logs and incidents (Art. 14, 26(2))
- **Systems (7):** CV screening and ranking, Targeted job advertising, Customer service performance analytics, Shift scheduling optimiser, Attrition-risk model, Pay review recommender, Internal mobility matching

### L03 · Decisions about people

- **For:** Recruiters, managers and HR business partners who decide
- **Covers:** Telling the person (Art. 26(11)), explaining a decision on request (Art. 86), human review
- **Systems (6):** CV screening and ranking, Customer service performance analytics, Shift scheduling optimiser, Attrition-risk model, Pay review recommender, Internal mobility matching

### L04 · Monitoring and workers' rights

- **For:** Line managers and HR operations
- **Covers:** Purpose and proportionality, what the data may and may not be used for, the national rules on telling workers
- **Systems (1):** Customer service performance analytics

### L05 · AI that talks to people

- **For:** The owners of chatbots and assistants
- **Covers:** Saying it is an AI, handing over to a person, what it must not answer
- **Systems (2):** Interview scheduling assistant, HR service chatbot

### L06 · Building a high-risk system

- **For:** The data and IT team that built it
- **Covers:** Provider duties (Art. 16), data governance, documentation, and why a retrained model is a significant change
- **Systems (1):** Attrition-risk model
